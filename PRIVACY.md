# Privacy policy

**QRH for Android** · effective 22 October 2026

**Developer:** Matthew Round. Contact: [qrh@matthewround.com](mailto:qrh@matthewround.com) or
[GitHub](https://github.com/anaes-dev/qrh-android/issues).

**Scope:** this policy covers only the app I build and publish on GitHub and Google Play. The source code is
shared under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), so others may
modify and distribute their own versions; those may handle data differently and are not covered
here.

**What I collect:** nothing. The app has no internet access, accounts, analytics or adverts, and
its settings stay on your device until you uninstall it.

**Google Play:** Google collects install and crash statistics under
[its own privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). I see only anonymous totals in the
Play Console, use them only to improve the app, and keep no copies.

**Children:** the app is intended for healthcare professionals and is not directed at children.

**Your rights:** as I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct
or delete. You can still contact me with any question, or complain to the
[ICO](https://ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

**Changes** are published here, with past versions kept in this repository's history.
